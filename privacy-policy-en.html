CloudHanbut Privacy Policy

Effective August 31, 2026

1. Overview

CloudHanbut (“the App”) is a puzzle game that does not require an account. The App processes the information described below to save game progress, operate the optional public leaderboard, and display ads.

2. Information and purposes

  • On device: score, stage, completion time, saved nickname, locally hidden reports, and advertising consent state are stored to maintain gameplay and settings.
  • Optional public leaderboard: when you choose to submit a record, the App sends your nickname, score, stage, completion time, and a random installation identifier. Nicknames and game records are visible to other players.
  • Advertising: the Google AdMob SDK may process IP address, device and app information, advertising or similar identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostic data for ad delivery, frequency control, fraud prevention, and consent compliance. Exact data depends on your device, region, settings, and Google policies.

The App does not request location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone access and does not directly collect your account name, phone number, or precise location.

3. Ads and children

Ad requests are configured for child-directed and under-age-of-consent treatment with a maximum G content rating. The App does not request Apple App Tracking Transparency permission for personalized advertising. Where required, Google’s consent flow is available and can be reopened from “Privacy & Ad Settings” in the App.

4. Service providers and international processing

  • Supabase: public leaderboard storage, retrieval, and reports. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: advertising, consent, and ad security. Google Privacy Policy and Advertising technologies

Information is transmitted over encrypted connections when these features are used and may be processed outside South Korea depending on provider infrastructure.

5. Retention and deletion

Public leaderboard records are kept while the service operates or until a deletion request is completed. Report information is retained as needed to prevent duplicate reports, address abuse, and resolve disputes, then deleted. Information may be retained separately when required by law. On-device data can be removed by deleting the App or resetting app data.

6. Public content and reporting

Nicknames must not include personal information, abuse, advertising, or content that infringes others’ rights. Use the flag button to report an inappropriate record and hide it immediately on your device. Repeatedly reported content may be hidden or removed.

7. Your choices and rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of a public leaderboard record by email. We may ask for the nickname and record details to verify the request. Ad choices can also be managed through in-App and operating-system privacy settings.

8. Security

We use encrypted transport, database access policies, disabled public table writes, server-side validation, and reporting tools. No transmission or storage method is completely secure.

9. Changes and contact

We will update this policy when features or legal requirements change and display the new effective date.
